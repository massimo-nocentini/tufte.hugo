{{< newthought >}}The wide right margin{{< /newthought >}} is the first thing a reader notices in a page set with Tufte CSS. It looks like wasted paper, until a note appears in it.{{< sidenote >}}This page sets `sections: none` in its front matter, so the whole text is a single `section` and no `h2` breaks it up.{{< /sidenote >}}

The margin keeps the text column narrow, around sixty-five characters per line, which is comfortable to read. It also gives notes a place where they can be read without losing one's place, and figures a place where they can be small without being cramped.

On a phone the margin collapses. Notes do not vanish: they fold behind their numbers and the &#8853; symbol,{{< marginnote >}}Like this margin note. Tap the symbol to open it.{{< /marginnote >}} waiting to be opened, and the text takes the whole screen.

White space is not empty. It is the part of the page that makes the rest of it legible.
