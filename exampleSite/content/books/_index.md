---
title: "Books"
description: "Multi-chapter books written with the tufte-book conventions."
---

A section whose `_index.md` sets `book: true` becomes a book in the style of
the `tufte-book` LaTeX class: a title page, a table of contents, lists of
figures and tables, numbered floats that continue across chapters,
cross-references between chapters, a bibliography and an index. Each book
below also has a single-page print view (`print.html`) for printing to PDF.
