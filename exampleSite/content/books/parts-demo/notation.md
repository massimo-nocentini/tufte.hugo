{{< xref "notation:intro" "full" >}}.
Equations {{< eqref "eq:euler" >}} and {{< eqref "eq:pythagoras" >}} use the usual notation.

In an appendix the theorems take the appendix letter:

{{< definition label="def:notation" >}}
$e$ is the base of the natural logarithm, $i$ the imaginary unit with
$i^2 = -1$, and $\pi$ the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.
{{< /definition >}}
