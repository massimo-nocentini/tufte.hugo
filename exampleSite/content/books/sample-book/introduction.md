This sample book discusses the design of Edward Tufte's
books{{< cite "tufte2001,tufte1990,tufte1997,tufte2006" >}}
and the use of the `tufte-book` and `tufte-handout` document classes,
here reproduced by a Hugo theme. The use of the book class itself is the
subject of the chapter “{{< xref "ch:tufte-book" >}}”.{{< marginnote >}}This book is a port of `sample-book.tex` from the [Tufte-LaTeX](https://github.com/Tufte-LaTeX/tufte-latex) project. Each chapter is a Markdown page of the section `content/books/sample-book/`, whose `_index.md` sets `book: true`.{{< /marginnote >}}
