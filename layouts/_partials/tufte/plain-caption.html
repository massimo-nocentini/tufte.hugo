The rest of the MathML copy and the visual HTML (span.katex-html, whose text is in layout order: \frac{a}{b} would read "ba") are left out. Also left out, as not part of the caption's wording: the toggles (label/input, "⊕") and the nested sidenotes, margin notes, notes in notes, citation blocks and real-footnote markers (span.tufte-fn) (spans balanced, so their own nested spans do not end them early). Cross-reference markers are kept as they are: the caller escapes them into the attribute and tufte/postprocess.html replaces the escaped marker with the resolved text ("Table 1"), as in the visible caption. A shortcode placeholder still in the caption (a shortcode in a Markdown image title, see tufte/figure.html) is dropped: it must not reach the attribute, where Hugo would put the shortcode's HTML.
