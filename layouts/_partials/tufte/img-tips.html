{{- /* Resolves the marked titles of images whose title held a shortcode: []TITLE The marks come from render-image.html (an image in running text) and tufte/cell-images.html (an image in a table cell). TITLE is the escaped title with the shortcode's HTML in it: its plain text becomes the img's title attribute (a tooltip) and its notes and citations follow the img (tufte/img-tip.html). Callers: func/content-raw.html (a shortcode written in the page's Markdown: Hugo puts its HTML in place after the hooks have run) and tufte/render-inner.html (a shortcode nested in a theme shortcode's inner text: a nest token, swapped there), each once the shortcode's HTML is in the title. Takes: the HTML (string or template.HTML). Returns: the HTML string. */ -}} {{- $html := string . -}} {{- if strings.Contains $html `` -}} {{- range findRESubmatch `(?s)(]*?)\s*(/>(?:)?)(.*?)` $html -}} {{- $tip := partial "tufte/img-tip.html" (index . 3) -}} {{- $img := index . 1 -}} {{- if and $tip.title (not (findRE `\stitle="` $img 1)) }}{{ $img = printf `%s title="%s"` $img $tip.title }}{{ end -}} {{- $html = replace $html (index . 0) (printf "%s%s%s" $img (index . 2) $tip.notes) 1 -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $html -}}
