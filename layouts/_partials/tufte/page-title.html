{{- /* The title of a page, translated where Hugo's own title is a fixed English string: 404 page i18n "pageNotFound" (Hugo: "404 Page not found") taxonomy page i18n key named after its plural ("tags", "categories"), when the title is Hugo's default (the title-cased plural: no content _index.md, or one without a title) and the key exists archive page i18n "archives", when the page has no title (layout: archive) Any other page, or a title set in front matter: .Title. Used by the and social titles (head.html), the h1 of taxonomy.html and archive.html, the back link of term.html and the RSS channel title. Takes: the page. Returns: the title (string). */ -}} {{- $t := .Title -}} {{- if eq .Kind "404" -}} {{- with partial "func/t.html" "pageNotFound" }}{{ $t = . }}{{ end -}} {{- else if eq .Kind "taxonomy" -}} {{- with .Data.Plural -}} {{- $plural := printf "%v" . -}} {{- if eq $t (title $plural) -}} {{- with partial "func/t.html" $plural }}{{ $t = . }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- else if and (not $t) (eq .Layout "archive") -}} {{- with partial "func/t.html" "archives" }}{{ $t = . }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $t -}}
