. In the whole-book print document (list.print.html, `print` true) every chapter is one article of a single HTML document, so the ids are made unique and the links turned into in-document anchors: - every id="X", for="X" and href="#X" gets the chapter prefix (tufte-cN-X); - links to any chapter of the book, href="REL#X" and href="REL", become href="#tufte-cM-X" and href="#tufte-cM"; - the output is wrapped in
, cN being the CSS named page that carries the chapter's running head. The ids are in the theme's tufte- namespace, so ids of the landing body (headings, labels), which keep their names, cannot collide with them. A chapter whose front matter sets `dedication` (Markdown) is a dedication page placed among the chapters (sample-book.tex puts it after the lists of figures and tables): instead of the chapter head it shows

TITLE

and the dedication markup of tufte/book/dedication.html, then its content (usually none).
