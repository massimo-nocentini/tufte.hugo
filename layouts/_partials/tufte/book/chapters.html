{{- /* The chapters of a book in reading order (SPEC-LATEX §4.1, R13). 1. Regular pages of the root, plus the regular pages of each part (a sub-section of the root; one level: pages of deeper sections are flattened into their part, with a tufte-book-structure warning). Pages under an unlisted section (`build: {list: never}`) are left out, as tufte/book/root.html leaves them out of the book, with a tufte-book-structure warning. 2. Sorted by group: a chapter at the root is a group of its own, a part is one group (its chapters stay together, as after \part in LaTeX). Groups sort by matter (front, main, appendix, back; a part's is that of its earliest chapter), then by weight (a part's weight, a loose chapter's own, so parts and loose chapters interleave by weight; weight 0 = unweighted = last), then by file name. Inside a part, its chapters sort by matter, weight, then file name. Never by date. 3. Num: 1-based index among main-matter chapters that are not `unnumbered`. Appendix chapters get the letters A, B, … as Display. 4. Numbered: book.numberChapters (resolved for the chapter) and not `unnumbered` and matter is main or appendix. Takes: the book root page. Returns: a slice of dicts { Page, Matter, Num, Display, Numbered, Part, Index, SortKey } (Part is the part page or false; Index is the 0-based position; SortKey is the reading-order key "GROUPMATTERRANK|GROUPWEIGHT|GROUPNAME|MATTERRANK|WEIGHT|NAME", names as their rank in tufte/book/name-ranks.html, which tufte/book/toc.html uses to place parts without chapters).
